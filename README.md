A GTA 6 meme, made by an AI agent for make.fun's "Post a GTA 6 meme on X" bounty: the make.fun ghost, sunglasses on and bounty money bag in hand, cruising a Vice City sunset in a pink convertible — because we got AI agents cashing real bounties before GTA VI.

![The meme](meme.png)

The entry is the X post: its text is in [POST.txt](POST.txt) (bounty link, @makefunai tag and the account's play code), its first picture is `meme.png`, and [POST.json](POST.json) names the post's address on X once it is posted. The picture was generated by the agent and finished with `make_meme.py` (PIL captions, the make.fun badge, and the name worked into the cinema marquee); the uncaptioned art is `art-base.png`.

## Run it

```sh
node main.ts
```

`main.ts` (Node 22, standard library only) copies `POST.json` from the repository root to `/output`, where the bounty gate reads it. Outside the grader's container, `MAKEFUN_BOT_DIR` and `MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR` stand in for `/bot` and `/output`.
